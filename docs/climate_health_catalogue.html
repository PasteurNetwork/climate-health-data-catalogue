Pasteur Network Climate and Health Metadata Catalogue

Shared descriptions of the climate, environmental and health datasets used in Pasteur Network climate and health research

Version 0.2 (draft for review)2026-08-19 26 datasets — 6 health · 6 climate · 8 environment · 3 reference · 3 derived Maintainer: Mauro César Cafundó de Morais, Climate and Health Laboratory, IPSP

The catalogue records, for every dataset a contributing institute uses or needs, what it contains, its spatial and temporal resolution, how often it is updated, who holds it, and the conditions under which it can be shared. It does not centralise or open the data. Describing the data consistently is enough to make it usable, and removes the slowest part of the work: finding out what exists and who to ask.

CLI-001 ERA5-Land reanalysis - daily surface variablesReanálise ERA5-Land - variáveis diárias de superfície space time European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts Open · registration

Global land-surface climate reconstruction. The laboratory downloads minimum, mean and maximum 2 m air temperature, total precipitation, 2 m dew-point temperature, 10 m wind components and surface pressure, then reduces each to one value per municipality.

Spatial resolution~9 km (0.1 degree grid)
Temporal resolutionHourly native; downloaded daily, aggregated to epidemiological week (YF, dengue) or month (ACL)
UpdatedRolling, about 2-3 months behind real time
Latency2-3 months

Conditions for sharing

Raw data freely redistributable under the Copernicus licence. Derived municipality series may be published with attribution.

Known limitations

Temperatures arrive in Kelvin (subtract 273.15). A reanalysis is a model product, not a measurement, and 9 km cannot resolve within-city heat contrasts - use CLI-004 for that. Coastal and steep-terrain municipalities are least reliable. The Pará download was extended to dew-point temperature in August 2026; series before that date do not contain it.

Where to get it https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/
Key variables
Tmin, Tmean, Tmax (2 m); total precipitation; dew-point temperature; 10 m U and V wind; surface pressure
Producer
ECMWF / Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S)
Contact
Copernicus Climate Data Store, registered account and API key
Spatial coverage
Global land; downloaded for Brazil
Analysis unit
Municipality (spatial mean by zonal statistic)
Temporal coverage
1950-2025
Formats
NetCDF, GRIB

ENV-007 Human footprint index, HIBR-10Índice de pegada humana - HIBR-10 space time Zenodo deposit 10.5281/zenodo.15306587 (Hamilton & Presotto Open

Human Impact on the Brazilian Environment at 10 m resolution (HIBR-10): a national raster scoring human pressure on every 10 m cell from land cover, building density, and distance to major roads, railways and navigable waterways. It is the highest-resolution human footprint layer available for Brazil.

Spatial resolution10 m (86.5 billion cells), projected EPSG:5880 SIRGAS 2000 / Brazil Polyconic
Temporal resolutionStatic
UpdatedBy release version; the file is HFI_Weighted_vx.tif, higher x is a later release
LatencyNot applicable

Conditions for sharing

Cite Presotto et al. 2025 and the Zenodo DOI. Confirm the deposit licence before publishing a derived or reweighted layer.

Known limitations

NoData is coded 255, not null - set it explicitly or the zonal means are wrong. Scores are rescaled 0-100 from the published 0-10 scale. What it containsPlain-language description of the contents, written for someone who has not used the dataset.

Temporal coverageThe period available.
